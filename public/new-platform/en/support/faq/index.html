Frequently asked questions

We’ve collected the most common questions to help you find answers quickly.

1. How many terms are in the diploma, and does the program follow two or three terms per year?
The number of terms and the academic calendar depend on the specific program. Check the program page or contact academic advising for the current calendar (two or three terms).
2. Do I receive an academic transcript when I graduate from the diploma?
Yes. An official transcript listing courses and grades is usually issued after graduation requirements are met, according to the awarding institution’s policy.
3. Do I have to attend live sessions daily with instructors, or are recordings enough?
Attendance rules vary by course and program (synchronous, recorded, or blended). Check the course syllabus and instructor announcements at the start of each term.
4. How do I access class links?
Links are usually posted on the course page on the platform, via email from the instructor, or in the calendar for live sessions.
5. Which application is used to join live classes?
It depends on institutional settings; sessions may run inside the LMS or through integrated video conferencing as configured for your program.
6. How do I access course materials?
Through the “Content” or “Resources” section on the course page after sign-in, which may include files, links, or recordings.
7. How can I get the midterm exam schedule for my employer?
Schedules are announced on the course home page or via official messages. You may request a formal document from academic affairs if available.
8. What are the courses in each diploma, their credit hours, and how are they spread across terms?
See the program catalog or program page on the website; study plans and credit totals may change from year to year.
9. How do I know my courses or schedule at the start of a term?
At the start of the term, check your student dashboard, internal messages, and course announcements; the schedule is updated officially by academics.
10. If I forgot my password, can support reset it for me?
Use the “Forgot password” page to receive a link or code. Support can help if you are locked out—passwords themselves are not shared.
11. Are diplomas for university graduates who work, or for high school graduates?
Admission rules differ by program. Review each diploma’s requirements on its program page (prior qualification, experience, etc.).
12. Does the certificate say “online,” “on campus,” or neither?
Wording follows official policy of the awarding body; refer to the certificate sample or accreditation section for your program.
13. Does the certificate say “intermediate diploma” or “associate”?
The qualification level follows official program accreditation and appears on the certificate text issued by the institution.
14. What grading scale or GPA appears on the diploma?
Grading rules are defined in academic regulations and appear on your transcript and certificate.
15. Does the diploma lead to professional certifications or recognition?
It depends on the program and industry partnerships; check the program page or relevant professional bodies for alignment with exams or licenses.
